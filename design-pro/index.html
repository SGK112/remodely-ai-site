Design Pro

Draw the room. Price the job. Send the fabrication plan.

A kitchen designer built by people who fabricate countertops, so it ends where the work actually starts — a cut list, a slab layout, and a number you can quote.

Open Design Pro → How to install it

Free to try. Runs in the browser — nothing to buy before you have drawn something.

What it does

1Draw the room

Walls, dimensions and ceiling height, or start from a room template. Snap and grid keep it square, and it validates against building standards as you go.

2Lay the cabinets

Real manufacturer catalogues with real SKUs — base, wall, tall, corner, lazy susan, sink, vanity, appliance. Pick a door and finish and the whole room follows.

3Pick the stone

Hundreds of quartz and natural stone surfaces from live vendor imagery, laid out against actual slab sizes rather than a swatch on a screen.

4Get the paperwork

A fabrication plan with cut list and cutouts, an itemised quote you can flip between retail and cost, and a design your customer can approve.

It installs like an app

Design Pro is a progressive web app. Open it once and your browser will offer to install it — then it has its own icon, opens without browser chrome, and keeps working when the signal drops on a job site. There is nothing to download from an app store and nothing to update by hand.

On a Mac or PCOpen it in Chrome or Edge, then use the install icon in the address bar.
On iPhone or iPadOpen in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
On AndroidOpen in Chrome, then Install app from the menu.

Catalogues come from the cloud

The cabinet lines, finishes and prices are not baked into the app. It fetches them from a versioned catalogue service each time it starts, so when a manufacturer changes a price list we publish the update and every installed copy picks it up — no reinstall, no new version to chase.

If the connection is gone, the app falls back to the catalogues it already has and keeps working. That is deliberate: a designer that stops working in a basement with no bars is not a designer anyone will use twice.

Bringing your own cabinet line is a spreadsheet, not a project. Send the SKU list — item number, description, width, height, depth, price, and the finish it belongs to — and it becomes a catalogue in the picker alongside the rest.
