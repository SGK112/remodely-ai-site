Billing questions

Can I cancel myself?

Yes — from your own billing page, no email required. Your embed stops serving; the leads you already have stay yours.

What happens if my card fails?

Stripe retries for several days and emails you. Your tools keep running the whole time — we don't cut you off over one bounced payment.

Do you take a cut of my jobs?

No. Flat subscription, nothing per lead, no revenue share. What you sell is entirely yours.

Who owns the leads?

You do. They go to your inbox. We don't market to them, and we don't sell the list — doing so would end this business.

Do I need a developer?

Only if you can't paste. If someone else runs your site, forward them the one line — it's a two-minute job.

Is there a contract?

No. Month to month, or annual if you'd rather pay once. Cancel whenever.
